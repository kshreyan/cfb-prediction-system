Read this before the numbers below: straight-up moneyline accuracy is expected to run 65–75% and is near-meaningless — most games are mismatches the market already prices correctly. The real tests are ATS record, CLV vs the closing line, and calibration. Any ATS/O–U record materially above ~54% is presumed leakage until investigated, not celebrated. This system currently shows no demonstrated edge over the market on any of the three targets — that is reported here as the correct, current result, not hedged away.
